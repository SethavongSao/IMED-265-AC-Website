1987 - 1991

Belkan Economic Crisis

Throughout the 1970's and 80's, the Belkan Federation started a series of research projects dedicated towards military superweapons and arms. The spending dedicated toward these military research projects caused the country to go into an economic recession. Over the years, they would begin selling off territory to their neighbors. Eventually, the country was taken over by a new government with an ultranationalist stance.

March 25th, 1995

Belka Begins Invasion

Natural resources were discovered in regions previously ceded to other countries by Belka. Wanting to reclaim these resources, Belka declared war on it's neighbors and began reconquering it's territory.

April, 1995

Allied Forces is Formed, Belkans Pushed Back

The nations surrounding Belka form the Allied Forces, and begin fighting back against the invasion. Ustio, who's airforce was destroyed by Belka in the initial fighting, starts to hire mercanaries to fight in it's ranks.

May 13th, 1995

Operation Constantine

The Allied Forces begin pushing Belka and is able to reclaim territory. This culminates in Operation Constantine, a decisive victory for the Allies that liberated the capital of Ustio.

May 17th-23rd, 1995

Allied Invasion of Belka

With the Belkans pushed out of their neighboring countries, the Allies begin pushing into Belkan territory. This culminates in the destruction of Belka's defense line and their superweapon, the Excalibur system laser tower.

June 1, 1995

Fall of Belka

The south of Belka falls and surrenders to the Allied Forces. The North and Central regions of Belka radicalized against the Allied Forces and continue fighting.

June 5th-6th, 1995

Nuclear Atrocity

The Belkan army detonates 7 nuclear warheads on the southern regions of their own country in order to stop the Allied advance. This results in the deaths of over 12,000 people and causes many soldiers within both Belka and the Allied forces to be disillusioned with the war.

June 19, 1995

Treaty of Lumen

Belka capitulates and agrees to terms set up by the Allied Forces. The peace terms greatly favor the Allied Forces and weakens Belka greatly. The Belkan War formerly ends here.

December 25th, 1995

A World With No Boundaries

After six months of peace, a terrorist organization known as "A World With No Boundaries" launch a coup d'état and hijacks the superweapon XB-0 Hresvelgr. The organization contained renegade soldiers from Osea, Sapin, Yuktobania and Belka who all believed that peace could be achieved if they dismantled the world's modern governments and national borders.

December 31st, 1995

End Of The Conflict

A World With No Boundaries takes control of Avalon Dam, a secret military facility containing missiles that they planned to use to strike cities around the world in order to plunge countries into anarchy, which would cause modern goverments to collapse. Belkan and Ustio forces are able to stop them, causing A World With No Boundaries to collapse and the missiles to destruct over a valley instead.
